The Breakfast Nook

Bathed in natural light, this cozy nook is a serene morning retreat. A Tiffany-style stained glass chandelier casts warm, colorful light over a solid wood dining set, while original windows with decorative corner rosettes overlook the lush, private backyard.

Built-in Millwork & Craftsmanship

Yesteryear craftsmanship shines in the kitchen's built-in hardwood hutch, with brass-pull drawers and glass-paneled upper doors for displaying fine china. An antique cabinet with ornate cast-iron hinges stands beside it, and a pocket doorway leads straight into the formal parlor.
